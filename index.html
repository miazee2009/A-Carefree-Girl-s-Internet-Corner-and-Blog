Boredom is no fun (literally). Here are some ideas for things to do when you're bored!

Read a book, draw, bake/cook something, make a website (like this one!), shop online, reorganize your closet, print out posters for your room, watch a comfort movie/tv show, learn to type quickly using Typingclub, work out, listen to music while doing chores, practice your posture by walking around with books on your head, watch a vlog, text (or better, call!) friends, journal.
